---

### Overview

This page explains how to install the Uninote AI app from the Zoom App Marketplace and connect your Zoom account to Uninote AI. This will enable Uninote AI to record, transcribe, and summarize your Zoom meetings.

### Step 1 — Install from the Zoom App Marketplace

1. Go to the [Zoom App Marketplace](https://marketplace.zoom.us) and search for **"Uninote AI."**
2. Click **"Add"** to install the integration.
3. Approve the connection. This grants Uninote AI the minimum permissions required to join meetings. For details, please refer to Permissions and Bot Identity.
4. Sign in to your [Uninote AI account](https://app.uninote.ai) to manage your settings.

### Step 2 — Connect Your Zoom Account within Uninote AI

1. Open Uninote AI and navigate to **Settings > Integrations.**
2. Click **"Connect Zoom."**
3. You will be redirected to the Zoom authorization page.
4. Review the requested permissions and click **"Allow."**
5. You will return to Uninote AI with your Zoom account connected.

> 💡 [HINT INFO]: Multiple members within an organization can independently connect their own Zoom accounts. Each member's authorization is unique to them and does not affect other members.
